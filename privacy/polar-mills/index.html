Privacy questions ↗

IS Interactive develops and publishes Polar Mills: Nine Men's Morris. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with the Game, including local gameplay, online multiplayer, advertising, and player support. It also explains the privacy choices available to players and how to contact IS Interactive.

1. Information We Process

The information involved depends on the features used, the Game's configuration, and your device settings.

  • Player identifiers. For online multiplayer, the Game may use account identifiers, product user identifiers, and available player profile information obtained through its configured authentication integration.
  • Multiplayer information. The Game uses session details, room information, connection information, and gameplay events required to create, join, and manage online matches.
  • Preset messages and reactions. When you use multiplayer communication features, the selected preset message or reaction sticker is delivered to the relevant player or players.
  • Local gameplay data. Game settings, preferences, statistics, replay history, and character unlock information may be stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising information. Where advertising is enabled, the Game's advertising integration may involve advertising identifiers, device and app information, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic data.
  • Technical information. The Game may use technical information needed to identify errors, troubleshoot connectivity, and maintain supported gameplay features.

2. How Information Is Used

IS Interactive uses information as necessary to operate Polar Mills and provide its features. These purposes include online authentication, multiplayer session management, gameplay functionality, delivering preset reactions, saving local settings, supporting advertising integrations, investigating technical problems, and responding to player requests.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Polar Mills integrates Google AdMob for advertising. When advertising is enabled, the Game may transmit information through the advertising integration, including device and advertising identifiers, application information, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostics.

Advertising functionality and available privacy controls depend on the version of the Game, applicable consent choices, device settings, and regional requirements.

For information about Google's separate privacy practices, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help Center .

4. Online Multiplayer — Epic Online Services

Polar Mills integrates Epic Online Services (EOS) to support Epic account authentication and online multiplayer functionality.

When you use online features, IS Interactive may access permitted player identifiers and available profile information through the Game's authentication integration. This information is used to identify players and enable supported multiplayer features.

The Game also uses information necessary to establish multiplayer connections, manage sessions, create and join rooms, and support online gameplay.

For information about Epic Games' separate privacy practices, please refer directly to the Epic Games Privacy Policy .

5. When Information Is Shared

IS Interactive uses third-party service integrations to enable certain features of Polar Mills. These include Epic Online Services for authentication and multiplayer functionality, and Google AdMob for advertising.

Information required for these integrations may be transmitted when the corresponding features are used. The categories of information involved depend on the Game's implementation and configuration.

During multiplayer sessions, selected preset messages and reaction stickers are shared with their intended recipients. Information necessary for shared gameplay may also be exchanged as part of the multiplayer session.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Certain Game settings, preferences, statistics, replay history, and unlock information are stored locally on your device.

This locally stored information generally remains available while the relevant application data exists. Clearing the Game's application storage or uninstalling the Game may remove this information.

Multiplayer-related information is used as necessary to support online sessions and related technical functionality. Any information retained directly by IS Interactive is kept only as long as necessary for its stated purpose or applicable legal obligations.

Communications sent to IS Interactive for support or privacy requests may be retained as necessary to respond to the request and maintain appropriate records.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

You may choose whether to use optional online multiplayer features. Certain information is necessary when those features are used.

You can manage some advertising and privacy preferences through available device settings and applicable consent controls.

Locally stored Game information may be removed by clearing application storage or uninstalling the Game. This does not necessarily remove information associated with external accounts or services.

To request access to or deletion of personal information controlled by IS Interactive, contact us at iqra818721@gmail.com . We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

For account privacy information relating directly to Epic Games, please consult the Epic Games Privacy Policy .

8. Children's Privacy

IS Interactive recognizes the importance of protecting children's personal information. We review privacy concerns involving younger players in accordance with applicable requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information relating to a child has been handled through the Game may contact us using the contact information below.

9. Data Security

IS Interactive takes reasonable precautions to protect information under its control against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

IS Interactive may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Game functionality, advertising integrations, online features, or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this website with a revised last-updated date.
