Account & Data Deletion Requests

This page explains how players can request deletion of data associated with Polar Mills, developed by IS Interactive.

How to submit a request

Email us with the subject "Polar Mills - Data Deletion Request". Include your in-game display name or another identifier needed to locate your game data. We may request reasonable verification before processing your request.

Never email your password, security codes, or other account credentials.

Request Data Deletion

What happens next?

We will review your request, verify that it relates to your account or data, and explain what information IS Interactive can delete or help you manage.

Some account information may be controlled by third-party services such as Epic Games. We will distinguish game-related data from data controlled by those providers.

Locally stored data

Some Polar Mills settings, statistics, replay history, and unlock information are stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage may erase this local data.
